Frequently Asked Questions

How does doorstep biometric verification work for leave and license agreements?
Once your agreement draft is finalized, our representative visits your home or office with an authorized biometric scanner linked to the government portal. Both parties scan thumbprints and verify credentials directly.
What happens if both parties cannot be present at the same location?
We offer a Corporate Split Package (₹3,499). Our representative completes the tenant's biometric verification at their office location and visits the landlord's residence separately.
Is police tenant intimation included in the total rent agreement charges?
Yes. Once your Leave & License agreement is approved by the government registrar, we generate and file the official Police Tenant Intimation receipt as part of our standard package.
What if a party is unavailable during the scheduled visit?
Due to any inconvenience caused from the client's side (e.g., party unavailable, left for work, or incorrect address), returning to the location for a re-visit will incur an additional operational charge of ₹300 per re-visit.
